SKYBOUND
RELIC: ASCENSION

Discover the lost history of the Skybound Kingdom across 20 levels. Latch onto towering walls and slide, wall-jump at any moment, and stomp flying wraiths to launch across impossible gaps. Each level reveals a piece of the relic’s story.

First three levels are tutorials: Jumping · Move combos · Enemies.

Keyboard and touchscreen controls supported. Double-tap the left or right arrow key to dash; on touchscreens, use the on-screen movement and dash buttons.
